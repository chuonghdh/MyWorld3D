# Brief: T-0027 (graphics-character)

- **Task id:** T-0027-ped-idle-micro-animation
- **Owner:** graphics-character
- **Reviewer:** reviewer (read-only subagent)
- **Goal:** standing people (pose STAND: waiting at the curb, looking at a shop window; `aGait.z == 1`) stop being frozen mannequins; seated (pose 3) and chatting (pose 2) people also breathe. All in the vertex shader (`applyRig` in `rigShaderGlsl.ts`, shared by far and near sets and by the depth material), zero CPU, no new attributes. Walking people are unchanged.
- **Design (lead's, change with a reason):**
  - Terms, all driven by `uTime` and the per-walker phase `aGait.y`, scaled by an `idle` factor (1 for stand, reduced for chat and sit as noted):
    1. Breathing: slow (about 0.25 Hz, per-person period +/-20 percent) chest rise: scale the BODY torso parts (part 0 region TOP, plus arms and head follow by a small `p.y` offset) by a few millimetres; shoulders lift a hair. Applies to stand, chat, sit.
    2. Weight shift: every 4 to 8 s the pelvis shifts a few millimetres sideways and one leg relaxes (thigh swing a few hundredths of a radian, opposite knee slightly bent). Stand only.
    3. Head glances: slow look left or right and slightly down or up by a few degrees (rotate the head parts about the neck pivot `uNeck`, same as the existing nod, adding yaw about the Y axis through the neck: add a `rotXZ`-like helper or reuse the existing pattern; the face is painted in the fragment shader from pre-rig position so it follows). Stand only.
    4. Arm settling: arms drift a few hundredths of a radian around the rest swing, plus a rare small hand fidget (forearm elbow +0.1 rad for 1 s). Stand only.
  - Hard limits: feet never leave the ground or slide (the legs rotate about the hip, so keep the thigh offsets small and compensate `p.y` if the pelvis drops); no limb penetration beyond the body (the amplitudes stay small by construction); two people at the same spot are out of sync (phase based hash from `aGait.y`). The idle terms must be exactly zero for walking people so the walk cycle stays bit-identical.
  - Keep this unit-testable: put the idle math in a pure TS function (e.g. `src/traffic/people/idleMath.ts`) that mirrors the GLSL (same constants), used by a test that sweeps time and phase and asserts bounded amplitudes (breath < 8 mm, shift < 10 mm, head yaw < 15 degrees, arm offset < 0.1 rad except the fidget) and that walking input gives zero. Document in both files that they must stay in sync; include the constants in one place if you can generate the GLSL from them (a template string with numbers from the TS constants is the best way to avoid drift).
  - Knobs (designer amplitudes) in `src/config/tuning/trafficLife.ts`: `idleBreathMm`, `idleShiftMm`, `idleGlanceDeg`, `idleArmRad`, each with a doc line, a parity entry in `tests/tuningParity.test.ts` and a row in `docs/TUNING.md` with the meaningful range. Uniforms feed the shader (shared uniform object like `uBlinkS`). Periods stay shader constants.
  - Shader cache keys: bump far (`person-rig-v4` -> `-v5`), near and depth keys, because the far and depth shaders change by design (the far program is no longer identical to before; update the `peopleLod.test.ts` assertions that pin the far key and the 'no near identifiers' check accordingly: far must still contain none of the NEAR identifiers).
  - Cost: a few dozen ALU per vertex for stand/sit/chat only (branch on pose; walking pays one branch). Report the rough instruction estimate in the handbook.
