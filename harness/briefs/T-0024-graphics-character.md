# Brief: T-0024 (graphics-character)

- **Task id:** T-0024-ped-near-face-shader
- **Owner:** graphics-character
- **Reviewer:** reviewer (read-only subagent)
- **Goal:** near people get a face drawn in the fragment shader instead of the eye, pupil, brow, mouth and moustache quads. The far set keeps its quads. This task also creates the NEAR MATERIAL VARIANT that T-0025 and T-0026 extend (near-only fragment work).
- **Design (lead's, change with a reason):**
  - Today near and far SHARE one material per age group (`createPersonMaterials(model, uniforms)` in peopleMeshes.ts). Make `createPersonMaterials` take a `near: boolean` option and build a second material pair for the near set: own `customProgramCacheKey` (e.g. `person-rig-near-v1`, depth `person-rig-depth-v4` can stay shared because depth ignores the face), a `#define PERSON_NEAR` injected into the shader prefix, GLSL for the face guarded by `#ifdef PERSON_NEAR`. Far material compiles to exactly today's program (same cache key as now). Update the night emissive list (`setNight`/`emissive` in peopleMeshes.ts) and dispose to cover the near materials. Keep every src file under 350 lines (split GLSL into a new file, e.g. `rigShaderFace.ts`).
  - Near geometry (`personShapeNear.ts`): remove the eye white, pupil, brow, mouth and moustache quads (`face()` helper and its calls). Keep the nose wedge and ears. Update the near triangle count assertions in `tests/people.test.ts` / `tests/peopleNear.test.ts` (range stays inside 900 to 1400; far unchanged and its checksums still pass).
  - Face in the fragment shader: only for the head part (part id 9, region SKIN) on its front side. Needs head-local info: pass `vPatPos` (model position, already a varying) plus per-model uniforms for head centre and radii (from `personDims.ts` via the model; add them to the model struct, same for every instance of an age group), and the instance's flags, hair colour, skin colour. Draw: eye white with an iris (iris colour from a small palette indexed by the instance phase, hash on `aLook`/phase, no knob), pupil with a highlight, upper lid line, brow in the hair colour (thickness and tilt vary a little per instance), nostril shadow under the nose, lips (colour from skin darkened, redder for women via `aLook2.z`), moustache when flag 1024 is set. Anti-aliased with `fwidth`-free smoothstep widths tied to radius (no derivatives needed). Must not paint on the back or sides: use the model-space z and the face normal (`dot(normal, forward)`) as a mask.
  - Blink and gaze: `uTime` and the walker phase (`aGait.y`): a blink every `trafficLife.blinkIntervalS` seconds (+/- 40 percent per instance by hash), lasting about 0.15 s (eyelid closes: eyes become skin colour with a lid line); gaze drift of the iris by a few percent of eye width, slow. New knob `blinkIntervalS` in `src/config/tuning/trafficLife.ts` with doc line, parity entry in `tests/tuningParity.test.ts`, row in `docs/TUNING.md`. Anything else (iris colours, sizes) is a shader constant and stays local.
  - Stooped elders: the head follows the neck nod, so face coordinates must be in head-local rigged space: use the model-space position BEFORE the rig rotation (vPatPos is `position` pre-rig: check) so the face moves with the head.
  - Seen from behind or from far in the near set (radius 30 m): fine, the face just reads as skin.
- **Verify by eye:** run `npm run smoke:closeup` (or its adult 3 m and 8 m shots, then child, elder, teen at 3 m), VIEW the PNGs and describe eyes, brows, mouth, any smearing or face on the wrong side. Report honestly if the face looks wrong and iterate (at most 3 iterations).
